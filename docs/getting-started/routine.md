# A useful monthly routine

Ledgerly becomes more useful when statement history is complete and categories are consistent. You do not need to do bookkeeping every day.

1. Export the latest original CSV statements from each relevant bank/card account.
2. Open **Import Statements** and inspect the import result, account and duplicate count.
3. Work through **Review** for unfamiliar merchant families.
4. In **Transactions**, inspect unusual entries and mark genuine one-offs.
5. Check transfer candidates and unmatched movements in **Reconciliation**.
6. Approve, update or ignore new **Recurring** candidates.
7. Check **Overview**, then review expected income and responsibility in **Plan** if circumstances changed.
8. Review **Goals** and **Allocate**. Paused or completed Funded Goals no longer need additional provision; target completion does not change status automatically.
9. Update **Wealth** snapshots periodically when asset values or liabilities change.
10. Use **Forecast** for a decision you have not yet made.

## Before asking for help with a number

Check the latest import, pending Review rows, unmatched transfers, one-off treatment, approved recurring types, selected household, Plan income and responsibility, and whether you are looking at a hypothetical scenario. Compare 3-month, 6-month and full-history lifestyle references before assuming a particularly expensive period is normal.

Keep an independent backup before upgrades or household deletion. See [backups and recovery](../backups.md) and [safe support diagnostics](../support.md).
