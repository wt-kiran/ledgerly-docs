# A worked monthly example

These fictional figures illustrate Ledgerly's planning relationships; they are not financial recommendations.

| Step | Monthly amount | Remaining capacity |
|---|---:|---:|
| Alex £3,000 + Jamie £2,000 expected income | £5,000 | £5,000 |
| Fixed commitments and variable essentials | −£2,000 | £3,000 |
| Regular saving/investing already in Plan | −£300 | £2,700 |
| Additional household savings target | −£200 | £2,500 |
| Active additional Funded Goal provision | −£600 | **£1,900 raw headroom** |
| Allocate lifestyle allowance | −£900 | £1,000 |
| Allocate extra investing | −£250 | **£750 safety buffer** |

## The Funded Goal

A £6,000 school-fees target with a ten-month deadline can reserve £600/month. Its Already saved amount stays unchanged unless updated manually. Its savings forecast still uses the separate monthly savings contribution. Pause or Complete removes the £600 additional reserve; merely reaching target does not.

If the same £600 was **already included** in a Plan commitment, choose Already covered by Plan and link that commitment. It adds no extra £600 reserve. If that existing commitment later falls to £400, the remaining £200 becomes additional provision automatically, so the full intended monthly commitment stays represented.

## A Tracking Goal

A £10,000 investment target with £4,000 saved and £200 monthly savings contribution shows £6,000 remaining and a 30-month savings projection. It creates **no additional headroom deduction**. If the £200 is genuinely a new commitment, record its funding in Plan or choose Funded deliberately; progress tracking alone does not reserve it.

## An actual periodic payment

When a provided-for bill is paid, it is still actual spending. Mark the payment one-off for normal-month planning when appropriate, or link the goal to an already modelled recurring bill. Do not leave both an independent provision and an independent monthly estimate for the same bill without checking for overlap.

## What if income falls?

Forecast uses the historical lifestyle reference, which may differ from Allocate's £900 allowance. If that reference is also £900, current after-lifestyle headroom is £1,000 (before Allocate's extra £250 investing). An income change of −£500 and lifestyle change of −£200 produce £700 after lifestyle. The scenario does not change the real Plan or the £750 allocation buffer until you deliberately edit those screens.
