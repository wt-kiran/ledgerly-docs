## What is Ledgerly?

Ledgerly helps you understand your household finances in one place.

You can import bank transactions, organise spending, manage recurring payments, plan your monthly budget, track financial goals and explore your financial outlook.

Your financial data is stored locally on your computer rather than in a Ledgerly-hosted cloud account.
